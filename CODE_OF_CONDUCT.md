# Code of Conduct

BLC Device Lab is a technical project. Discussions, issues, reviews, and test reports should stay respectful, evidence-driven, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Critique code, designs, test results, and evidence rather than people.
- Give others room to correct mistakes and ask questions.
- Keep reports relevant and avoid spam or low-effort bulk submissions.
- Respect device privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, malicious disruption, or publishing private or security-sensitive information without permission are not acceptable.

Do not publish another person's device identifiers, ADB credentials, wireless-debugging pairing material, private captures, logs containing secrets, or other sensitive device data.

## Security issues

Potential vulnerabilities should be reported according to `SECURITY.md` rather than disclosed in a public issue before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
